      How to Use PokeTools

      Quick visual guide to planning Pokémon video challenges & runs

      1. Pokédex Scrambler

      Enter any Pokédex number or Pokémon name to find every valid Pokémon formed by rearranging its digits. By default, numbers are padded to 4 digits so Gen 9 Pokémon (#1000–#1025) are included: for example, entering 123 scrambles 0123 to reveal #0123 Scyther, #0132 Ditto, #0213 Shuckle, #0231 Phanpy, #0312 Minun, #0321 Wailord, and #1023 Iron Crown.

      2. Lettermon Builder

      Type any Pokémon name (using live autosuggest) or enter a custom spelling/nickname to view all Attacks, Abilities, and Items starting with each letter in the name. Toggle "Learnable by this Pokémon only" to restrict moves and abilities to that Pokémon's actual learnset, or click any letter pill in the sticky bar to focus on a single letter.

      3. Global Filters & Settings

      Click Filters in the top-right bar (or the side tab on the right edge) to restrict results across both tools by Generation (1–9), Legendary/Mythical status, Max BST, or Specific Game Regional Dex. Expand Advanced Settings at the bottom of the drawer for Type filters, Megas & Regional Forms, 3-digit/exact scrambler padding, and your preferred Pokédex reference site.
